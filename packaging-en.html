2023

Context

This project involved the development of a packaging line for adaptogenic products built around original digital illustrations. Each product received a unique illustration designed to communicate its individual identity within the collection while maintaining a clear visual relationship with the rest of the line.

Rather than using illustration as a decorative element, the goal was to make it one of the central components of the graphic system. The illustrations serve as visual identifiers for each product and help create a cohesive, distinctive, and memorable visual identity across the collection.

Project Challenges ↓

01

Applied Illustration

Developing illustrations capable of functioning both as standalone pieces and as integrated elements of the final packaging.

02

Product Differentiation

Creating individual identities for each product without losing the sense of belonging to the same collection.

03

Cohesive Visual System

Maintaining consistency across illustrations, composition, and visual hierarchy.
