An illustrated collection with its own identity within a cohesive visual family.

Context

Illustration as a recognition system

Original digital illustrations build the recognition system for this line of adaptogenic product packaging. Each package receives a distinct image that communicates its own identity while maintaining a clear visual relationship and sense of belonging across the collection.

Brief

The project in three decisions

Approach

Developing illustrations capable of functioning both as standalone pieces and as integrated elements of the final packaging.

Differentiation

Creating individual identities for each product without losing the sense of belonging to the same collection.

Consistency

Maintaining consistency across illustrations, composition, and visual hierarchy.

Process

From illustration to packaging

Visual studies showing the development of the illustrations later used across the packaging line.

Result

Four products, one visual family

Each package uses its own illustration as a point of recognition, while composition and hierarchy maintain the collection's visual relationship.
