About

Hello!

I'm a graphic designer, illustrator, and recent psychology graduate. I enjoy applying design to different purposes, which is why many of my projects end up moving between design, illustration, research, and science communication.

Curiosity has guided much of my professional and academic journey. Over time, I discovered that learning across different disciplines did not take me away from my interests. Instead, it gave me more tools to approach them from different perspectives. Today, I particularly enjoy working at the intersection of design, research, and communication.

What can I say? I like to be ready for anything.
