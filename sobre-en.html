Hello!

I'm a graphic designer, illustrator, and recent psychology graduate. My areas of study are more than credentials; I enjoy applying what I've learned across different disciplines to create well-rounded solutions. I don't see myself as someone who simply executes ideas, but as someone who genuinely cares about developing them.

Curiosity has been a driving force throughout my academic and professional journey. I don't like limiting myself to a single discipline because I enjoy discovering the intersections where different fields come together. My design professors often emphasized that design can be found everywhere. At the same time, I've come to believe that design itself becomes stronger when enriched by knowledge from different disciplines.

What can I say? I like to be ready for anything.
