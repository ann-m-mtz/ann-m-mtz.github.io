A less tedious, more playful way to decide what to eat.

Context

The question we always go around in circles about

“What are we going to eat?” used to start arguments at home, with my family, and in my relationship.

Process

From early sketches to an identity of its own

The identity was developed primarily to work as a recognizable identifier for the application.

Condensed brief

A recipe book for when no one wants to decide

Objective

Create a solution containing all the “safe” meal choices for days when no one wants to think about what to eat and there is no time, desire, or energy to try something new.

Audience

People who need a recipe book and, above all, a way to decide what to eat without turning the conversation into an endless loop of “whatever you want.”

Personality

It keeps the traditional look and feel of a recipe book while integrating contemporary functions. It takes the classic family recipe book and turns it into something a digital native would want to use.

What did I do?

Concept development, name selection (naming), creative direction, identity development (wordmark and symbol), color palette, type selection, product design, and UX/UI design.

Result

Two essential ways to get to the food

A simple app with two essential modes: recipe book and craving duel.
